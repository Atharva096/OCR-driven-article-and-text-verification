Major Subsea Cable Upgrade Completed in APAC Region

Date: 2026-11-07

Source: Global Tech Daily

Singapore-based telecom giant OceanConnect announced today the successful completion of a major subsea cable upgrade linking Singapore and Tokyo. The $500 million infrastructure project will increase bandwidth capacity by 40%. This merger of legacy networks solidifies OceanConnect's position in the telecommunications domain and is expected to impact regional data routing significantly.
